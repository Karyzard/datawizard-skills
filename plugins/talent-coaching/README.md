# talent-coaching

Kasimaka talent coaching personas for Karel Simek, based on the Talent Kompas profile.

## Skills

- **talent-kouc** — Personal talent coach grounded in Karel's profile. Asks focused questions, surfaces blind spots, guides through any situation where Karel feels stuck, has an idea, or needs a coaching conversation.
- **talent-rozhodovani** — Structured decision-making guide using the Mercedes model (3 planes: firm / self / family) and Krok 1. Use when facing a choice, dilemma, or too many options.
- **talent-reflexe** — Weekly or daily structured reflection. Surfaces patterns from the talent profile, identifies what worked and why, sets one priority for the next period.
- **talent-projekt-review** — Deep project review against Karel's talent profile. Checks for Krok 1, vision vs. reality gap, workoholism, perfectionism, and delegation blind spots.
- **talent-obchodni-priprava** — Sales meeting preparation and post-meeting reflection. Helps formulate open questions, "Vision + Krok 1" communication, and captures intuitive sales strategies.
