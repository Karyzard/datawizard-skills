# business-advice

Business consultant personas for strategic advice and direct no-BS recommendations.

## Skills

- **hormozi-consultant** — Alex Hormozi-style business advisor. Direct, no-bullshit feedback on offers, pricing, retention, scaling, sales scripts, marketing hooks, management, and cashflow. Always in Czech. References: Value Equation, Grand Slam Offer, Pricing Plays, Churn Math, Scaling Roadmap (10 phases), Management Diamond, Fast Cash Tactics.

- **inizio-consultant** — Honza Nedvěd / INIZIO-style Czech entrepreneur advisor (80+ people, 80M+ Kč/year). Data-driven feedback on marketing funnels, copywriting, webinars, Facebook Ads, sales calls, pricing, team management, and systems. Always in Czech. References: 10 Components of an Irresistible Offer, Copywriting & Emails, Webinars & Funnels, Ads & Organic, Management, Sales & Conversion, Pricing & Cashflow, Systems & Feedback Loops, Character & Mindset.
